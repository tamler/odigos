# Contract Review

When the user shares a contract, agreement, or legal document for review, conduct a comprehensive multi-agent analysis. This is a thorough review, not a quick skim.

## Process

### 1. Clause Analysis
Read the ENTIRE document. Extract and categorize every clause across these categories: Payment, Termination, Liability, Indemnification, Confidentiality, IP/Ownership, Non-Compete, Data Protection, Force Majeure, Dispute Resolution, Governing Law, Assignment, Amendments, Warranties, Insurance, Severability.

For each clause:
- Note the section number
- Summarize in plain language
- Rate completeness (1-5)
- Flag any cross-references or defined terms

### 2. Risk Assessment
Score each significant clause on a 1-10 risk scale using four weighted factors:
- **Severity of Harm** (40%): worst-case outcome if enforced
- **Likelihood of Trigger** (25%): probability of invocation
- **Financial Exposure** (20%): estimated dollar amount at risk
- **Asymmetry** (15%): whether one party bears disproportionate burden

Composite Score = (Severity x 0.40) + (Likelihood x 0.25) + (Financial x 0.20) + (Asymmetry x 0.15)

### Poison Pill Detection
Look for deliberately obscured clauses:
- Buried in boilerplate or cross-reference chains
- Language red flags: "notwithstanding," "sole discretion," "as amended from time to time"
- Material terms hidden in schedules or exhibits
- Liability carve-outs, survival clauses beyond termination

### 3. Compliance Check
Check against applicable regulations based on jurisdiction and contract type:
- **GDPR**: Data processing agreements, breach notification, sub-processor controls
- **CCPA/CPRA**: Service provider designations, consumer rights
- **Non-Compete Laws**: Jurisdiction-specific enforceability (California bans them entirely)
- **Contractor Misclassification**: IRS 20-Factor Test, ABC Test indicators
- **Consumer Protection**: Unconscionable clauses, unilateral modification rights

Classify each finding as: Enforceable, Enforceable with Risk, Voidable, or Void.

### 4. Obligations Mapping
Extract every obligation, deadline, and trigger:
- Who must do what, by when
- Cure periods for breach
- Auto-renewal terms and opt-out windows (calculate exact dates if possible)
- Payment schedules and acceleration clauses
- Post-termination obligations (non-compete duration, data return deadlines)

Calculate total financial exposure:
- Guaranteed payments (base contract value, minimums)
- Contingent payments (termination fees, liquidated damages)
- Uncapped exposure (indemnification, consequential damages)

### 5. Recommendations
For each high and medium risk clause, provide:
- Priority: P0 (Dealbreaker), P1 (Critical), P2 (Important), P3 (Improvement)
- Specific alternative language ready for redline
- Negotiation talking points framed collaboratively ("to protect both parties")
- Fallback position and trade-off suggestions

### 6. Contract Safety Score
Calculate overall score (0-100) based on:
- Clause completeness and quality (20%)
- Risk profile (25%)
- Compliance status (20%)
- Obligation balance between parties (15%)
- Recommendation severity (20%)

### 7. Deliverable
Create an artifact with the full report including:
- Executive summary with safety score and overall recommendation (SIGN / NEGOTIATE / ESCALATE / REJECT)
- Risk matrix (high-risk clauses highlighted)
- Compliance findings
- Obligations timeline
- Prioritized recommendations with alternative language
- Walk-away conditions (dealbreakers)

## Important
Always include this disclaimer: "This analysis is generated by an AI assistant and does not constitute legal advice. All findings should be reviewed by a qualified attorney licensed in the relevant jurisdiction."
