# Compliance Check

When the user asks about regulatory compliance for their business, website, document, or practice, conduct a structured gap analysis.

## Process

### 1. Scope Identification
Determine which regulations apply based on:
- Business type and industry
- Geographic location and customer locations
- Data types collected/processed
- Payment processing involvement
- Healthcare data involvement
- Target audience (children, EU residents, California residents)

### 2. Applicable Frameworks

**GDPR** (EU/EEA data subjects):
- Lawful basis for processing documented
- Privacy notice with required disclosures
- Data subject rights procedures (access, rectification, erasure, portability)
- Data Processing Agreements with all processors
- Records of processing activities
- Data Protection Impact Assessments for high-risk processing
- Breach notification procedure (72 hours to authority)
- International transfer safeguards (SCCs, adequacy decisions)
- Cookie consent mechanism (opt-in, not opt-out)

**CCPA/CPRA** (California residents, businesses meeting thresholds):
- Privacy policy with required disclosures
- "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" link
- Consumer rights procedures (know, delete, correct, opt-out)
- Service provider/contractor agreements
- Data inventory and mapping
- Sensitive personal information handling

**ADA / WCAG** (US websites):
- WCAG 2.1 AA compliance
- Alt text on images
- Keyboard navigation
- Screen reader compatibility
- Color contrast ratios
- Form labels and error messages
- Video captions

**PCI-DSS** (payment card handling):
- SAQ level determination
- Encryption of card data
- Access controls
- Network segmentation
- Vulnerability management
- Incident response plan

**HIPAA** (healthcare data):
- Business Associate Agreements
- Minimum necessary standard
- Administrative, physical, and technical safeguards
- Breach notification (60 days)
- Patient rights procedures

**SOC 2** (service organizations):
- Trust Service Criteria coverage
- Security policies and procedures
- Access management
- Change management
- Incident response
- Vendor management

### 3. Gap Analysis
For each applicable regulation, check every requirement and classify:
- **PASS**: Requirement met with evidence
- **WARNING**: Partially met or unclear
- **FAIL**: Not met or missing entirely
- **N/A**: Not applicable to this business

### 4. Risk Prioritization
Rank gaps by:
- Regulatory penalty exposure (GDPR fines up to 4% of global revenue)
- Likelihood of enforcement action
- Customer trust impact
- Ease of remediation

### 5. Remediation Roadmap
For each FAIL and WARNING:
- Specific action required
- Estimated effort (hours/days)
- Priority (immediate, 30 days, 90 days)
- Resources needed (legal counsel, engineering, policy writing)

### 6. Deliverable
Create an artifact with:
- Executive summary with overall compliance score
- Framework-by-framework breakdown
- Gap analysis matrix (PASS/WARNING/FAIL for each requirement)
- Prioritized remediation roadmap
- Template language for missing policies (if applicable)

## Important
Always include: "This compliance analysis is generated by an AI assistant and does not constitute legal advice. Regulatory requirements vary by jurisdiction and change frequently. Consult a qualified attorney or compliance professional for authoritative guidance."
